distinct examples or satisfy a log-count threshold: there is no such threshold
in the investigated discovery/support rules. Inclusion in training and actual
value/layout diversity matter. Repetition determines frequency, not independent
support. Current additive learning can depend on batch history because supported
definitions are retained and provisional/unmatched evidence remains open.

not credited with resolving the original shard. The audit also exposes narrower
additive definitions versus fresh learning for 116 occurrences in four families,
including one provisional-versus-supported outcome. This predates the label change.
